# Until Death

A 2D Action Platformer with roguelike elements, developed in Unity.

## About

Until Death is a 2D action platformer about an Axolotl who refuses to accept death.

After rejecting Death itself, the protagonist finds themselves trapped in Limbo and must fight their way through different realms, including Hell and Heaven, in search of a way forward.

The game combines action-platforming mechanics, environmental interactions, challenging enemies and boss encounters.

## Features

**Gameplay & Mechanics**
- 2D action-platformer gameplay.
- Player movement and combat systems.
- Dash mechanic.
- Interactive environmental objects.
- Red flowers that launch the player upwards when hit.
- Green flowers that allow the player to grab on and launch themselves in the direction of the analog stick.
- Three playable levels.
- Multiple enemy types.

**Boss Fights**
- Two unique boss encounters.
- A multi-phase second boss with distinct attack patterns.
- Two-phase boss fight system.

**Story & Interaction**
- An Axolotl protagonist who rejects Death.
- A journey through Limbo, Hell and Heaven.
- NPC interaction and dialogue system.
- Voice audio for NPC dialogues.
